About Samaritan Web Tech Services

Samaritan Web Tech Services is a property technology platform dedicated to simplifying the rental experience for landlords and tenants. The platform offers a secure, stress-free environment for property listings, tenant screening, digital lease agreements, and automated rent tracking.

By bridging the gap between landlords and reliable tenants through smart technology, Samaritan helps property owners manage rentals efficiently, reduce vacancies, and grow their income. The system provides an easy-to-use, secure, and reliable solution with dedicated support, focusing on making property management uncomplicated and growth-oriented.

Core Services Include:

  • Property Advertising
  • Maintenance Management
  • Rent Collection & Automation
  • Centralized Digital Hub Management
